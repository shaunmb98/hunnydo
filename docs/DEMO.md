# Five-minute demo outline

Use the deployed app and a fresh account so the onboarding is on screen.

**0:00 Why (30 seconds).** The landing page. Tell the water line story: hours of calls, the same explanation over and over, quotes and schedules to keep straight, and nowhere to keep the good plumber afterward.

**0:30 Sign up and onboard (40 seconds).** Create an account. Type your address, pick it from the list, and watch the eight trades fill in. Point out the county records row (year built, square footage) and the top-ranked pro per trade.

**1:10 Something broke (60 seconds).** Upload the real photo of the water pooling by the meter box. Add one sentence. While it works, say what it is doing. When the diagnosis lands, show the urgency banner, "Do this now", the cost range, and the job brief. Edit one line of the brief to show it is yours.

**2:10 Who to call (45 seconds).** Open "Pros and quotes". Show the match scores and the reasons under each. Press "Check who shows up" and let the scores move. Pick three and press "Write my quote request".

**2:55 Quotes (60 seconds).** Show the text, email and call script for one pro. Mark one as contacted. Paste a messy reply into "Log their quote" and show the fields it pulled out. Add a second quote. Open the side by side view and press "Help me choose".

**3:55 Hire and keep (35 seconds).** Book one. Show the booked summary, the note for the pros you passed on, and the confirmation email. Open My crew and show the plumber saved there. Open My house.

**4:30 The other side (20 seconds).** Switch to "My business", show the job board, and bid on a job as a pro.

**4:50 Close (10 seconds).** What you would build next: calls placed by a voice agent, replies read straight from your inbox, and a phone app.
