## Short answer

No single product was found that combines a photo diagnosis, a ranking nobody can pay for, a homeowner-owned quote board, a lasting crew list and a house file. Each piece exists somewhere. Thumbtack is the closest single product.

## The real threat

Google. One secondary source says its AI calling expanded to plumbing, HVAC, electrical and roofing across the US in summer 2026. That is the feature HunnyDo plans next. It was not confirmed on Google's own pages.

## The name

- No app, business or trademark turned up for "HunnyDo" or "Hunny Do". This was a web search, not a trademark office search.
- "Honey Do" is crowded in handyman services: The Honey Do Service (a registered franchise), Honey-Do Handyman (registered), a pending 2024 "HONEYDO" application in building repair, and others.
- There are household reminder apps named HoneyDo and HoneyDoo.
- A name that sounds the same in a related class is a question for a trademark lawyer before spending on the brand.
